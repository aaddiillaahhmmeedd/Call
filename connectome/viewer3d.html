How it is assembled

Brain regions and streamlines: diffusion MRI of one adult (OpenNeuro ds000114 sub-01), in that scan's space. Cord grey matter and tract paths: PAM50 template at true size, attached at the lowest brainstem slice. Cord tractography: a second person's cervical scan, shifted onto C1–C5. Brainstem nuclei, cerebellum, cranial nerves and peripheral pools are placed schematically; each node's panel says which.
